three floors), eight real minutes a round, as a human would: read the tape and the dot, hover a glowing chamber,

## What a player does in GROW, in three sentences

Click a glowing chamber and choose which of four organs it becomes, reading the gauges (the dot names what the body
lacks, the tape names the organ) against the price (the room's own organ is cheap). Pump the heart in rhythm to fill the
take: each beat sends a red drop down the body into it, on the beat four times as much. Keep the four gauges green as the
floors get deeper and dearer, take the machine for its hands, dream when you want the years to run, and rise.
