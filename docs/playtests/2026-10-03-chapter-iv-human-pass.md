## C. The five worst moments

1. **GROW has no real choice.** Mass went from 29 to 224 and never stopped me; no gauge went red, nothing starved, the tape never named an organ. I picked the green cheap one every time and it was always fine. Fix: make mass tight so the cheap organ can be the wrong one, let the tape name the missing organ every few takes, and let an ignored PULSE kill the edge within a minute.
2. **Pumping is clicking one spot 10 to 18 times per chamber,** about 12 chambers in 8 minutes. Fix: 3 or 4 heavier pumps per take that only count on the beat, or hold to pump with a rhythm to keep.
3. **TEND has no crisis,** then a 57 second WAIT for ★15 K with three lamps lit. Fix: a real stall in the first two minutes (power short when the second farm starts), and drop the star lamp or make the wait something to spend.
4. **The long sleep is a shopping loop.** Asleep, the tape says WAKE whenever any level is affordable; awake earns 400 000 times less; six queued "lv mine" in a row; a FAULT wake with no cause; prices like 1.1e15. Fix: wake only for a reason the screen shows, sleep long, and plain numbers.
5. **The drawer hides the colony.** "EMPTY 1" and "BUILD MINE" while the empty chamber sits under the drawer. Fix: pan the scene left when the drawer opens.
