## Grind and dead moments found, and what I did
- Every deep dive was 30 s of transport (fall + climb through 300 tiles). Climbing now accelerates, falling already did.
- After all six rows were bought (sim minute 14 at the old prices) parts piled up with nothing to buy: 19 minutes of digging for nothing. Raised level 3 to 400 and the warm rock softer; the last row is now bought around minute 18, then grafts at 21 to 22, heart at 24 to 25. Late ore still matters: it feeds the colony, whose drain grows with time.
- The sim player stalled at 900 m with all ore above mined and no parts for HULL 2: a player who spends everything on lamps can strand. Deep ore is now richer, and the workshop text names each gate's depth so a player can save for it. Not fully proof against a player who buys everything else first.
- First dive kills a beginner who just holds down (battery 40). Kept the danger (it is the core decision), added the home mark and the turn-back line.
