# Chapter IV · THE DEEP · the rebuild (2026-10-03)

Written by Claude at Ola's request after he played IV through: "Misslyckad nivå hittills.
Coola detaljer men fast i någon gammal tanke. Man klickar och det fladdrar, läser massa
text och försöker fatta. Ingen känsla av upp och ner. Ingen känsla av att man kommer in i
en annan värld där det biologiska spelar någon roll. Ingen känsla av Unity eller the body.
Släppa på tyglarna, ta inspiration från det vi gjort tidigare men hitta en egen vinkel."
This document replaces the earlier IV docs as the plan. They stay as history. No em-dashes.

## Ola's brief (2026-10-03, verbatim)

The humans have gone underground to wait out the storms that rage above. The surface is
dead and has to revive. It will take millions of years to do so. Man has to survive. The
player plays as a Watcher that looks over the humans. First priority is to sustain life;
we need resources for that. Mine them. Next, we need time. To get it we need to put our
humans to cryo sleep. But time is also the enemy: through the millennia the Watcher's mind
goes. It starts to hallucinate. Other options arise to save the humans. Do they need their
bodies? Do we need them? Options to start using their bodies to sustain and to feed the
machines. To meld man and machine. In the end the Deep is one huge body, ready to conquer
the earth, to reclaim its dominance over it. "Humans are so small. So fragile." Gameplay
finished and polished from start to finish. Great graphics with sound and music. Goal: to
evolve from machine to flesh and escape the depth. The DEEP of the earth and the DEEP of
time should be felt in the player's body and mind. Sync sound and music with previous Acts.

## The angle

Chapters I to III each have one verb and the screen tells you what it is. IV had six
verbs on one screen and explained them in sentences. The rebuild gives IV three verbs, one
at a time, and lets the SCREEN change with each: what you look at becomes what you are.

| movement | you are | verb | the screen | the crisis | ends when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I · TEND | the machine | build | an instrument panel over a lit colony | hunger and power: needles fall into red | the three lamps of cryo are lit and you pull the lever |
| II · SLEEP | the mind | wait, and hold on | the colony dark, a counter of years, your own pulse | madness: hallucinations, a reboot | the voice asks the question |
| III · GROW | the body | spread | the panel overgrown into organs, the colony turning to flesh | the body's hunger: people run out, flesh dies back | the body fills the floor and breaks the crust |

The economy underneath (ore, food, energy, people, the machine's stars, cryo tiers, the
healing surface, END_YEAR) stays: it is tested and simulated. What changes is what the
player sees and does with it.

## I · TEND (the machine)

- **The instrument panel** replaces the four bars, the advisor feed and the readouts:
  four round gauges with needles, left side, labelled on dymo tape: ORE, FOOD, POWER, HANDS.
  The needle shows days of cover (or the flow when nothing falls); the red arc is "runs out
  soon". Under them one stamped label, the only advice in the game: "INSTRUMENTS: BUILD FARM"
  (or DIG, or AUTOMATE MINES, or SLEEP). It changes rarely and never explains itself.
- **Empty chambers are visible**: a dug chamber without a room is a plate with a faint
  "+" outline in the scene, and the panel shows a counter "EMPTY 2". Clicking an empty
  plate offers the four rooms right there (a small ring of four icons with prices).
- **Buying lives where it acts**: rooms on their plates; levels and automation in a side
  DRAWER (not a full-screen board) that slides in from the right over a quarter of the
  screen, so the colony stays visible behind it. The drawer shows ONLY what can be bought
  NOW (bright) and the NEXT thing per branch (dim, with what it needs). Nothing else. The
  full tree is not the main interface any more; it can be opened from the drawer as "the
  whole tree" for those who want the map.
- **Cryo is three lamps**: a small lamp row on the panel, each lamp a condition (FOOD
  RUNS ITSELF, POWER RUNS ITSELF, ORE RUNS ITSELF). Lit = done. When all three are lit, the
  big lever appears: SLEEP. No sentence lists requirements.
- Crisis and cascade: early stalls (food or power falls to red) and the cascade of the first
  automation, when a whole needle swings to green at once.

## II · SLEEP (the mind)

- **Pulling the lever**: the panel lights go off one by one, the colony darkens, people
  walk into the cryo hall, the sound goes under water (already built), and the counter
  takes the centre of the screen: "THE COLONY HAS SLEPT" over a large year count that rolls
  like the odometer, with the surface's healing as a thin ring around it.
- **Time accelerates by itself** within a sleep (slow at first, then faster, by the tier),
  so every sleep is a dive. The deeper the tier, the steeper the dive.
- **Waking has one reason, shown as one lamp**: a single alarm lamp lights with its word on
  dymo tape: FOOD, POWER, FAULT, VOICE or AWAKE (you woke them). The panel comes back on.
  No sentence.
- **The mind goes** (the Watcher's stability, unchanged underneath): as it falls, the screen
  HALLUCINATES: a lamp burns in an empty chamber, a figure stands on the crust, a needle
  points where nothing is, a year digit reads wrong, the walls of a plate breathe once.
  Clicking the base snaps it back (the existing snap) and the false things vanish. A reboot
  wakes everyone and costs a sleep's worth of years.
- **The voice is the hallucination.** Surface appears only in the sleep, only when the mind
  is below a threshold, and types its lines (the existing six). Its rock, paper, scissors
  card vanishes 6 s after the result. From night 4 on, its letters are drawn in the same
  style as the Watcher's own label; by night 6 the two labels merge: SURFACE becomes
  WATCHER for a moment, then the question.

## III · GROW (the body)

- **The question opens the body.** The panel is overgrown over ten seconds: a vein crosses
  the glass of each gauge, the dymo labels peel and are replaced by organ names in a warmer
  hand: ORE becomes MASS, FOOD becomes FEED, POWER becomes PULSE, HANDS becomes FLESH. The
  needles become fluid levels. The drawer becomes tissue (the locked mockup 12 look:
  mycelium, sinew, vessels, red pulse; no mushrooms, no sacs).
- **The body spreads as a front on the same floor.** The lid plate becomes the first organ.
  Every plate that touches the body glows faintly as reachable; click one and the flesh
  takes it over about two seconds: the slab turns from light concrete to dark wet tissue
  (a procedural material: veins, ribbing, a slow pulse), its people walk into it and do not
  come out, its output multiplies. The front only grows into neighbours, so the body is
  always one connected thing. When a floor is full, the shaft becomes a spine and the next
  floor opens below.
- **The hunger.** Flesh eats people every year. When FEED is empty, flesh at the edge of
  the body greys and stops (necrosis) until people return. Dormitories that the body takes
  become VATS: they grow people instead of housing them. Uphill (hunger), downhill (a vat
  ring feeding a floor), uphill again (the next floor is hungrier).
- **The machine becomes hands.** As the body reaches the machine house, the three tube
  heads are overgrown and become hands: a fist, a flat hand, two fingers. The game was
  always a game of hands. Stars are still its wins.
- **The rise.** When the body fills the deepest floor and the machine is hands, the lever
  returns, overgrown: RISE. The body pushes up through the shaft and the crust. The last
  lines: "Humans are so small." pause "So fragile." Then the V card. The surface ring does
  not matter to the body: it does not need the air. That is the reversal.

## GROW, second pass (Ola on v1.76.0, 2026-10-03)

Ola: "The question: we must make the consequences clearer. Maybe we taste a little bio

### Diagnosis (Claude)
GROW arrives as a cliff: one purchase, then a full body mechanic with a cost that empties
the colony before the player has seen one benefit. The player never chooses flesh; it
happens to them. And nothing on screen says what a flesh room is FOR.

### The fix: three steps down instead of one cliff
1. **A TASTE, before the question (SLEEP, from night 4).** Surface's fourth gift becomes
   a GRAFT: the player may turn ONE chamber of their choice into flesh. It is a single
   organ that cannot spread. On taking it, the room's output visibly multiplies (a
   floating "×5" over the plate and the gauge needle jumps), it eats a few people a year,
   and nothing else changes. The player has now seen the trade with their own eyes.
   Night 5 offers a SECOND graft. The grafts are where the flesh look first appears.
2. **THE QUESTION as an informed choice.** Its drawer row and the dymo advice say what it
   does in one line each, not more: "The body takes the colony, room by room." /
   "It eats people. It grows them in vats." / "It is the only way up." It is a choice:
   it never has to be bought, and the panel says what the other road is ("THE PEOPLE
   WAIT. SURVIVAL 85 % AROUND YEAR 802 701" as before, if the player keeps sleeping).
3. **GROW with a feeding loop the player controls.**
   - Vats come FIRST: the question gives the body two vats at once (the culture vats
     the player bought become the body's), so FEED is rising when the body starts.
   - The first takes are cheap and eat little; hunger scales with the body, not with
     the clock. Taking a chamber never costs more people than FEED can spare: if a take
     would starve the body, its price shows red and the dymo says "GROW VATS".
   - Starvation is a slope, not a wall: necrosis greys the edge slowly and the body
     keeps producing from its heart; a necrotic room revives on its own as soon as FEED
     is above zero. There is ALWAYS a doable action shown by the dymo: GROW VATS (buyable
     with MASS, which the body keeps making), FEED (pause spreading), or SPREAD.
   - What flesh does, shown every time: on each take, the plate shows the multiplier
     ("×20") and the MASS needle moves; the lamp row shows the road to the rise as counts:
     "DEEPEST FLOOR 3 / 12", "MACHINE 0 / 1".
   - **Show what you pay with.** Ola: "It costs people but it doesn't say how many you
     have." People get a third counter top right beside ore and stars (a person glyph, the
     count, "+N a day" or "-N a day" under it), from TEND on, and the FEED gauge carries
     the number. Every price in people is next to that count.
   - **One choice at a time.** Ola: "It should come more gradually, not every choice at
     once." GROW opens with ONE verb: take a chamber. Each next thing appears only when it
     is needed, the chapter II teaser way: VATS when FEED first falls; APPETITE (how much a
     chamber eats) after the first necrosis; SPREAD (the flesh takes on its own) after ten
     chambers by hand; MUSCLE after the first floor is full; the machine's hands when the
     front reaches the machine house. The drawer never shows a body item before its moment.
4. **Sim rule:** the scripted player must reach the rise WITHOUT ever being unable to
   act for more than 10 real seconds; the sim reports "longest stuck" for GROW and the
   act stays 25 to 35 minutes.
