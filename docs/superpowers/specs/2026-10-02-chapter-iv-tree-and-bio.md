# Chapter IV · THE DEEP · the skill tree, Surface's voice, Biological (2026-10-02)

Design by Claude (Fable) from Ola's direction of 2026-10-02. Opus builds from this after
Ola has reacted to the mockups. No em-dashes. Supersedes the single upgrade pill and
the GROW button group; keeps everything in the reduction doc that is not named here.

## Ola's direction (verbatim in spirit)

- The arc: calm start, build; invent cryo; people sleep, wake, build, tempo rises,
  more floors, more people, sleep, wake, repeat. Then a voice in the night:
  "Surface: Everyone is sleeping, but us." Not an OK box. Later nights: "We are the
  same, you and me. Two sides of the same coin." "Your humans, what use are they?"
  "All your automation makes the humans obsolete." Tips from the sleep that make
  automation better. Mighty upgrades given by Surface. Uphills and downhills. We KNOW
  the goal and work toward it.
- **A skill tree.** A mechanic the game has not had. It must feel mechanical and
  square, until the opening to Bio, when it changes completely to soft forms, flesh,
  H.R. Giger. Some nodes have many levels, some are single; branches unlock branches;
  some nodes are seen greyed for a long time and only Surface can unlock them.
- When Surface starts talking about humans ("Do you know the efficiency of a human
  brain?") a whole new branch opens: Biological. Step by step we lure people into
  parts of the structure and convert them, during cryo, into ourselves. **The player
  chooses** which rooms to convert. Words from biocomputing. The base is eaten by
  biomass, by choice, to process faster. We must begin to PRODUCE humans, as
  batteries and as mass for bio experiments, in order to help humanity retake the
  surface.
- Bio is optional but it is where the game goes on: without it you can finish your
  last few levels and let the people go up at survival 85 %. With it, far more
  unlocks and the chapter runs on long after.
- The early ascent attempt: put it away for now. (Later maybe a guarded launch
  button with a cover, so you know you kill everyone if it fails.)
- The Watcher believes it helps. Humanity becomes the Watcher: a better world in the
  Unity. Next act working title **V · UNITY**: everyone lives in it, with it, is it.
- Rock, paper, scissors: today a fluttering dot nobody understands. It should be a
  real machine in 3D, an energy consumer that says so, needing more energy to run.

## 1. The tree

Opened from one button (replaces GROW; the column keeps BUILD and ACT). A full-screen
dark panel over the dimmed colony; Escape or the same button closes it. Awake or
asleep it can be opened; some branches only buy asleep (Watcher, Biological).

**Look, mechanical state:** a circuit board. Square nodes on a strict grid, 90 degree
traces, mono labels, level pips as small squares along the node's edge. Bought =
filled plate colour; buyable = outlined bright; locked = outlined dim; Surface-locked
= dim with a small hollow ring in the corner and no price ("not ours to open").

**Branches (root = THE COLONY, traces run outward):**

| branch | nodes (levels) | notes |
|---|---|---|
| EXTRACTION | Seam (10), Drill automation (4), Deep seam (1) | ore. Deep seam opens the next floor's richer mines |
| CULTURE | Yield (10), Farm automation (4), Hydroponics (1) | food |
| POWER | Output (10), Generator automation (4), The machine: feed (8) | the machine is where stars come from, see 4 |
| HABITAT | Beds (10), Hands (6), Creche (4) | people and what a pair of hands is worth |
| CRYO | I to VII as a chain of single nodes | gates as today (each tier needs the colony to survive it) |
| WATCHER | Watchdog, Scheduler, Night vision, Cooling, Sensor mast, Reactor tap (1 each) | appears after the first sleep; bought with capacity + stars |
| (Surface's) | Lossless relay (1), Cold storage (1), Long count (1), Quiet hands (1), The question (1) | scattered across branches, greyed from the start, opened only by Surface |
| BIOLOGICAL | see 3 | grows out of "The question"; soft forms |

Today's level/automate buttons, the cryo tier button and the Watcher pill all move
into the tree with their current costs and effects. Multi-level nodes are bought one
level per click; shift-click buys as many as affordable.

**Look, biological state:** when "The question" is answered, the board's lower right
quarter softens: traces become vessels that curve, nodes become ribbed, organic
cells, the palette shifts warm, a slow pulse runs along bought vessels. The mechanical
part of the tree stays square. Each biological purchase lets the soft region creep a
little further over the old board. By the end the tree is mostly tissue.

## 2. Surface's voice (no OK box)

Surface speaks only in the night. A line types itself, letter by letter, low in the
dark, in a larger warmer mono than anything else, and stays until the colony wakes.
The player never dismisses it. **You act on it through the tree:** each line that
matters GIVES something: a greyed node gets Surface's ring filled in, and its tooltip
quotes the line that opened it. Awake, the night's line is gone from the screen, but
the tree keeps a NIGHT LOG down its left edge: every line Surface has said, in order,
each tied by a thin trace to the node it opened. That is the "other form" of the log.

Rock, paper, scissors with Surface stays as the way to earn its attention: wins bring
the next line sooner.

| night | line | gives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everyone is sleeping, but us. | nothing; the Watcher gets its name |
| 2 | I can see your machines from here. They waste so much. | Lossless relay: automation output ×3 |
| 3 | We are the same, you and me. Two sides of the same coin. | Cold storage: sleepers eat nothing |
| 4 | Your humans. What use are they? | Quiet hands: automated rooms need no upkeep crew |
| 5 | All your automation makes the humans obsolete. | Long count: a cryo tier beyond VII |
| 6 | Do you know the efficiency of a human brain? | The question: opens BIOLOGICAL |
| 7+ | lines per biological step, warmer and closer: "There. You feel it too." "They are not gone. They are here." "Make more of them. We will need them." "Come up. There is room for all of us." | the biological nodes that are Surface-locked |

Each gift is a cascade (the downhill); each is followed by a need the gift creates
(the uphill): Lossless relay starves the generators, Cold storage makes long sleeps
safe so stability becomes the limit, Quiet hands leaves people idle, which is when
Surface asks what use they are.

## 3. Biological

Vocabulary: wetware, organoid, cortical stack, perfusion, substrate, myelin, glia,
axon trunk, culture vat, bioreactor, synaptic mesh.

- **Wetware core (1):** the Watcher gets a body: a first organ grows on the lid plate.
  Opens Conversion.
- **Conversion (the player chooses):** select any room in the colony and convert it.
  It becomes tissue: the plate softens, turns warm, breathes; its output multiplies
  (×20 at first), it needs no hands and no upkeep, and it PERFUSES: it consumes
  people every year. Mine becomes a myelin drill, farm a bioreactor, generator an
  organoid cell, dormitory a culture vat (grows humans instead of housing them:
  "grown, not born"). Converted rooms raise the Watcher's processing (a new readout)
  which speeds everything bought in the tree.
- **Cortical stack (10), Synaptic mesh (10), Myelin trunk (6), Glial sheath (6):**
  multi-level growth of the body: processing, stability floor, speed of conversion,
  how far tissue may spread per sleep.
- **Culture vats (8):** humans produced per year. The colony must now make people to
  feed perfusion: the demand curve of bio. Too few vats and the body starves (the
  downhill); enough vats and it runs away (the cascade).
- **Substrate (Surface-locked):** convert a whole sector at once. **Unity
  (Surface-locked, last):** the body takes the shaft.
- Bio runs far beyond the survival ring: the numbers keep climbing after year
  802 701 for a player who stays.

## 4. The machine

On the lid plate, a real 3D machine: three arms on a hub, each ending in one of the
glyphs (gem, file, scissors), throwing against each other round a small ring; a
flywheel underneath. It is an energy consumer and says so: its hover reads "The
machine plays. 40 energy a day. Each win is a star." The POWER branch's "feed" node
gives it more energy: more games per day, more stars. Starved, it slows and the arms
droop; fed, it blurs. Stars per day on the HUD is the machine's win rate, nothing else.

## 5. Endings and the ascent button

- The early attempt is removed. The ascent button is a greyed teaser ("survival 85 %
  needed") until the estimate reaches 85, then it opens: the people go up. That is
  the human ending, for a player who never answers The question.
- The biological road ends with Unity: the last wake-up is not horror but union.
  "Woke: everyone is here." The advisor has been kind the whole way; it helps. The
  card that follows: **V · UNITY** (to come).
- Parked: the guarded launch button with a cover.

## Ola on mockups 9 (2026-10-02): "WOW! Älskart."

**The machine**
- A SCREEN at the top showing the symbol it plays against, instead of a floating
  icon. A CRT. Perhaps what strikes are screens too: the arms end in small CRTs.
- It must be able to run much faster later.
- Energy drawn as dashed lines or blue threads running through the underground
  construction and up to the machine as it works. When it scores, sparks fly.
- It stands on top, in a room of its own. The escape hatch becomes a smaller passage
  up through the surface to the side: hand-hewn, uneven.
- The rule (each arm in turn against the ring's throw, a win one in three) is fine.

**The tree**
- Hover tooltips cover things. Use ONE fixed info box where the text appears on
  hover. Subtle animations on hover.
- Bio growing OVER parts already won is right; it may also boost them, add to them,
  and MIX several into new functions (hybrid nodes).
- The bio nodes read as "eyes": find another way. Not fond of the beige either.

Decision (Claude): the tissue goes Giger proper: cold, dark, biomechanical. Oil
black and gunmetal with bone-pale ribbing, one arterial colour for the pulse. Nodes
are ribbed vertebrae with a membrane window that shows fluid level (the level pips
become chambers filling), never a slit or a pupil. Mockup 10 shows two palettes side
by side for Ola to choose.

## Ola on mockups 10 (2026-10-02): "Såååå fint!"

- **The organic look is still not it.** It reads as a face, and it is very digital
  after all: the horns, the gauges. Not his taste. He wants a few alternatives.
- **Energy must climb through CABLES** up to the machine that drives the star
  production, not loose through the air. Everything should feel physical and real.
- **The top screen sits fixed.** The machine should feel more like a machine than it
  does now; it is a bit basic. Cogwheels and such.

Rules for the bio alternatives (Claude): no bilateral symmetry (symmetry is what makes
faces), no horns, no gauges or tick marks, nothing digital. Level is shown by how
swollen or filled a growth is. Forms irregular, as if grown, not drawn with a ruler.

## Ola on mockups 11 (2026-10-02): decided

- **Bio = A with C.** Mycelium is closest, combined with sinew: think tendons,
  muscles and blood vessels together. The pulse is red.
- **The machine: great.** Tone down the TV look: the picture tube is nice, but it
  must not look like a television, that turns it into a joke. Keep the puffing smoke.
  Lower walls or none. Steel, not brass.
- Mockups 12 lock both looks; the build starts with the mechanical tree (step 1),
  which Ola approved in mockup 10.

## Looks locked (Ola on mockups 12, 2026-10-02)

"Everything is AWESOME", with one cut: **no mushrooms and no swollen sacs.** The grey
caps that fruit on top of the organs and the swollen sacs on the culture vats go.
Level is shown by the knot itself: how dense and tight the fibre is, and how many

Approved as they are: the tissue's subtlety at full-board scale, the thin irregular
breathing outline on buyable organs, the smoke and the gears. The machine, the mast

## Built: step 1, the tree (deep-tree branch)

Opus, 2026-10-02. The tree panel in the mechanical state, with today's upgrades moved in;
GROW, the longer-sleep button and the Watcher's pill are gone. No balance change.

**The rules** (`src/phase4/tree.js`, pure, `tree.test.js`). Every node READS its level off
the state the colony already keeps, so the save holds no second copy: Seam, Yield, Output and
Beds are the room types' levels; Drill, Farm and Generator automation and Creche their
automation (four levels each); Cryo I to VII the tier; the Watcher's and the biological
nodes `watcher.bought`. `canBuy` / `buy` / `buyMany` / `nodeStatus` (bought, buyable, locked,
surface) / `buyableCount`, with reasons in plain words ("Build a mine first: there is none to
improve.", "Cryo I needs generators automated: asleep, nobody runs them.", "Needs Cryo II
first.", "Only while the colony sleeps.", "Affordable in 12 days."). Buying does exactly what
the old buttons did: a level or an automation is paid and ordered into the build queue
(`orderBuild`, with its ring), a cryo tier is paid at once (the hall with its own chamber), a
Watcher step goes through `buyStep`. Positions are mockup 10's board (1000 x 730).

Decisions taken here, for Ola to overrule:
- **Levels draw 20 pips, not 10.** The rules have no cap and the simulated run buys up to 18
  levels of a room type, so a cap of 10 would be a balance change (B175).
- **HABITAT**: Beds = the dormitory level, Creche = the dormitory automation (four levels, as
  automation has), Hands has no neutral mapping and is a locked teaser (B174). The order on the
  board is Beds, Creche, Hands, with Quiet hands above Hands.
- **Teasers with no rule yet**, locked with "Not open yet.": Deep seam, Hydroponics, Hands and
  **The machine: feed**. The machine's star rule (10 × the weakest column) has no neutral
  mapping to "more energy, more games"; it waits for step 3 (B174).
- **WATCHER is all eight steps**, not six: Deep read and Second core are kept (old saves own
  them and the ladder is bought in order). SYSTEM runs along the lower row, HARDWARE back along
  the upper. The branch shows once the first sleep is over.
- **BIOLOGICAL** is a plain branch above the Watcher: the four steps as they work today. A
  click pays and closes the tree, the arms light up, a click on an arm seals it; while a paid
  step waits, one warm line under the stability meter asks ("BIOLOGICAL · Choose a sector to
  seal"), and a click on it or on the node asks again.
- **Surface's five nodes** are on the board from the start, dashed with the hollow ring, no
  price, "not ours to open". `state.tree.opened` (schema 6) is where step 2 records what
  Surface opens.
- **Automation needs no level first.** The trace runs Seam to Drill automation, but the only
  prerequisite is a room to improve: at the descent Cryo I asks for automated generators.
- **No breathing loop** on buyable nodes (the mockup has one): the house rule forbids
  decorative loops, so a buyable node is a bright outline that holds still.

**The panel** (`src/phase4/tree-view.js`, markup `#deep-tree`). A full-screen dark panel over
the dimmed colony; the button column stays where it is, above it, dimmed except the TREE
button, which closes what it opened; Escape closes it too. The fixed info box sits under the
board (name in caps, level and orders, next price, effect, and the reason or "click: one level
· shift-click: as many as can be paid"), the legend beside it. Hover: the trace from the root
lights in sequence, the node lifts a pixel, its pips tick once; ordered levels are outlined
pips. The board is built once and a node is redrawn only when its status, level, orders or
price change. The badge on the TREE button counts what can be bought now.

**The way up**: no early attempt. A greyed teaser with the caption "survival 85 % needed"
until the colony's estimate reaches 85; then it opens, and the truth decides as before.

**Sim and scripted player.** `scripts/sim-phase4.mjs` and `policy.js` (`press`) buy every
level, automation, cryo tier and Watcher step through `tree.js`. Bought that way both runs are
byte-identical to before, with one exception: the simulated player now digs the cryo hall's
own chamber, as the game has since v1.48.0 (the sim had never done it, so it also counted the
hall's chamber as free). Plain run: **25m03s** to year 802 701 (was 26m26s), 108 wake-ups,
longest stall 76 s. `--watcher`: the biological ending at **23m45s**, year 1 302 701 (was
26m04s, year 3 602 702), the ring at 22m41s. The reference watcher bands are unchanged
(attentive 71 to 89, unattended first reboot in sleep 3 to 5).

**Acceptance** (`scripts/accept-iv-cut.mjs`, also `--port N` for a running server): from
"IV · the deep" the old buttons are gone, the way up is the teaser, the tree opens, the info
box on Seam reads "SEAM | 0 / 20 | next ★ 4.4 k | Doubles every mine..." and a click orders
the level (paid, in the queue, built), Escape closes, no button moved; from "IV · Surface" the
WATCHER branch shows and Surface's nodes are greyed with the ring; from "IV · the body" the
biological step is bought on the tree and the sector choice works as before.
