# Chapter IV · THE DEEP · what actually works, and the cut (2026-09-28)

Written by Claude (Fable) at Ola's request after his playtests of v1.48 to v1.51: "take a
pass and think about what actually works here, with my earlier comments in mind."
Opus builds from this. No em-dashes.

## The diagnosis in one paragraph

Chapter IV got every layer at once. The sleep world alone now asks for attention from
a stability meter, a capacity bar, one or two riddles, Surface with a game and a
sentence, a twelve-step upgrade ladder, a build queue, an advisor feed and a replay
strip. Chapters I to III never do that: they show one thing, tease the next, and let
the player discover. The awake world has the same fault in the button column. Nothing
is wrong with any single piece; the fault is that they all speak at once, so the player
reads none of them ("so much info I do not understand, crowding, begging for
attention"). The cure is hierarchy and sequence, not more features.

## What works (keep, do not touch)

- Sleep as a state with alarms; the odometer; the dark colony with indicator lamps.
- The survival ring and the one word "survival"; scout parties with odds.
- Stores with flows and the dot on what runs out; hover previews; build times.
- The base softening and the snap. Ola's own note: one thing to idle-click to keep
  sanity up is enough. The snap IS that button.
- Surface: the visit, the game, the sentence. It is the best thing in the sleep world.
- The two endings.

## What does not work (and why)

1. **Riddles.** Hard, dull, and two at once with Second core. They compete with the
   snap and with Surface. Nobody asked for maths in the dark.
2. **The ladder as twelve buttons.** Twelve near-identical round buttons in a column
   read as one button. Ola could not find BIOLOGICAL because it looked like the rest.
3. **BIOLOGICAL has no picture and no choice.** A step is bought, a sector is sealed
   by the code, the feed says one calm line. Nothing on screen changes that the player
   chose or can see, so it feels broken.
4. **Capacity** is a second currency nobody understands, shown as a bar with no name.
5. **Too many voices:** advisor, replay strip, Surface line, result line, upgrade
   tooltips, alarms. All mono, all the same size, all at once.

## The cut (slice 9)

**Rule: in the sleep world, one demand at a time.** A scheduler decides what may be on
screen: Surface visit OR a lamp sequence OR nothing. Never two.

1. **Sanity = the snap, and only the snap.** Remove riddles from the default loop. The
   base softens, the player clicks it, it snaps: that is the whole idle mechanic, as
   Ola asked. Stability drift and snap gain tuned so an attentive player holds 60 to
   90 and an absent one drifts to a reboot in a few sleeps. Lamps (the sequence game)
   become an occasional EVENT, at most once per two sleeps, announced by the lamps
   themselves, never while Surface is there. Number riddles: gone. "Deep read" and
   "Second core" get new effects (snap +10; lamps give double).
2. **Capacity goes invisible.** No bar. It is still earned by the machines while
   asleep, but it is only ever shown inside the ONE upgrade pill: "needs 120 · 84 now".
3. **The ladder is one pill.** Below the Watcher, a single wide pill shows the NEXT
   step only: rung name in small caps, step name, cost, one line of effect:
   "SYSTEM · Watchdog · 120 cap + 5 k ★ · stability drifts slower". Above it a thin
   line with three ticks (SYSTEM, HARDWARE, BIOLOGICAL) and a filled trail, so the
   whole road is visible and the next rung's name is readable long before it opens.
   Buying advances the pill. When BIOLOGICAL opens, the pill turns warm and the line
   reads what it takes: "BIOLOGICAL · Brain tissue · 24 people". That is where the
   biological thing is, and it cannot be missed.
4. **BIOLOGICAL is a choice and a picture.** Buying a biological step does not seal a
   sector by itself: the pill asks for a sector, the arms of the base light up as
   choices, the player clicks one, and THAT arm turns warm, breathes, empties of
   walkers, and the count of people drops visibly on the H bar with a red delta. The
   Watcher glyph itself changes per biological step (a dot, then a dot with a ring,
   then a soft blob, then a blob with a rim), so the entity is seen to grow. Sealed
   sectors keep producing; the tooltip on a sealed plate says "part of the body".
5. **Voices, ranked.** Asleep: the alarm line (when it fires), Surface (when it
   visits), the upgrade pill. That is all. The advisor feed collapses to its last line
   while asleep; the replay strip appears only at the wake-up and only for five
   seconds; result lines live inside the Surface card. Awake: advisor feed (3 lines),
   bars, queue strip, buttons in three groups (slice 8).
6. **Surface untouched** except that its visit pauses everything else.

## Not now (parked)

More Surface lines, more lamp variants, sealed rooms changing output, the confined
areas as a map overlay, sound. Only after Ola says the cut reads.

## Acceptance

From "IV · Surface": sleep, and at no time are two demands on screen; the base can be
kept rigid by clicking; the next upgrade is always one readable pill; from "IV · the
body": buying a biological step asks for a sector, the chosen arm changes visibly,
people fall visibly, the Watcher glyph changes. A playtester who has not read this
document can say, after five minutes, what the snap does, where the biological
upgrade is, and what it costs.
